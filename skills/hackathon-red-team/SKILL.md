# Hackathon red team

Use at least product, technical, skeptical, and rule/sponsor perspectives for
important reviews. Separate blocking findings from improvements, identify test
gaps, and create prioritized repair tasks. A meta-judge reconciles disagreement
without erasing minority evidence.

Never weaken a rule gate or hide a failing check to declare readiness. Re-run
the affected verification after repair and preserve the before/after finding.
